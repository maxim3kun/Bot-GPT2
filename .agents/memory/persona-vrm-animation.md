Use Alicia Solid as the VRM model for personas. Keep it distinct from Maxime's own personalized avatar.

VRM provides a humanoid model and rig, not a complete movement library. Full-body actions such as walking, jumping, typing, and expressive gestures require compatible animation clips, authored motion, or retargeting to the model's skeleton.

**Why:** The user wants Alicia Solid to remain available for personas and wants those characters to perform full-body actions, including jumping.

**How to apply:** Keep persona and Maxime avatar choices separate. Test and tune motion on the chosen VRM rig, and be clear when a movement is only a simulated pose rather than a complete animation.
