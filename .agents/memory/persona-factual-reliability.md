---
name: Persona factual reliability
description: User expectations for persona roles, factual estimates, and source relevance.
---

Personas must preserve their configured role when answering factual questions. They should give a supported answer or acknowledge that they do not know, rather than automatically behaving like a scientific expert and inventing figures. Sources should appear when useful to justify an answer, not on ordinary conversation merely for decoration.

**Why:** The user reported unsupported carbon-emission estimates and country multipliers from a conversational persona, and explicitly preferred a truthful admission of uncertainty to a confident incorrect answer.

**How to apply:** Keep personality separate from factual authority. Use research for evidence-dependent estimates, distinguish assumptions and units, and explain missing evidence in the persona's own voice. Do not treat old assistant answers as verified facts.
