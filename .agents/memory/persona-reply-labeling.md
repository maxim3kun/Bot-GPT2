name: Persona reply labeling
description: The user's preference for how persona replies are identified in Discord.
---

Do not add an “AI-generated” label to persona replies; Discord already identifies the account as a bot.

**Why:** The user explicitly asked to omit the extra AI label because people can already see that the Discord account is a bot.

Persona names and images are per-user; do not change the shared bot account nickname or avatar when one user activates a persona.

**Why:** A shared Discord account cannot show different actual names to different users in the same server; changing it for one persona would affect everyone.

**How to apply:** Keep replies under the bot account and show the selected persona name and image in its reply. Do not add an AI label or claim the persona is its creator or a human.
