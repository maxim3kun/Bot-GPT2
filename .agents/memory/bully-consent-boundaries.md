---
name: Bully consent boundaries
description: Consent rules for the server-shared Bully persona.
---

Bully may direct sharp remarks only at the member who activated it. Do not roast another member, even when the active user asks; mentions, quotes, and screenshots are not reliable proof of consent. Stop the persona or its biting tone immediately when the active user asks.

**Why:** The user requires voluntary activation and bars remarks toward members who have not agreed. The bot has no reliable per-member consent signal for third-party targeting, so refusing all third-party roasts avoids guessing.

**How to apply:** Keep Bully output bound to the current author and their session. Any future feature for consensual third-party teasing must first collect and verify that person's explicit consent.
